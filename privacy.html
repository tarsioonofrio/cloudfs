Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how cloudfs (the “app”), a personal Linux client that mounts a Google Drive account as a local folder, handles information. In short: the app runs only on your own computer and the developer does not receive, store or see your data.

1. Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app can access your Google Drive through the Google Drive API using the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. This includes the names, folder structure, metadata and content of your files. This access is needed so that the app can show your Drive as a folder and read, create, modify, move and trash files when you do so through that folder.

2. Where information is stored

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) are stored in a file on your own computer, readable only by your user account.
  • File metadata and cached file content are stored in a local database and cache directory on your own computer.

No information is stored on any server operated by the developer. The app has no developer-operated servers.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the app's features that you request: listing, reading, writing, renaming, moving and deleting your Drive files, and keeping the local view up to date. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or to train any machine-learning model.

4. Sharing and disclosure

The app does not share, sell, transfer or disclose your information to the developer or to any third party. The only network communication is directly between your computer and Google's APIs. No humans read your data.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

cloudfs's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Run gdrived logout to delete the stored token from your computer.
  • Delete the app's local data directory to remove all cached metadata and file content.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because the developer does not hold any of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

7. Security

Tokens are stored with owner-only file permissions, and communication with Google uses HTTPS. You are responsible for the security of your own computer and user account.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/tarsioonofrio/cloudfs/issues.
